About Us

A staffing partner built specifically for cleaning work.

Nixora Services LLC provides specialized cleaning personnel for large-scale events and for industrial facilities and environments. Our service model is simple: supply a committed, trained workforce that delivers efficient, reliable, high-quality cleaning while meeting the most rigorous safety standards and requirements.

Proprietary talent network

Immediate access to an exclusive, pre-screened pool of experienced cleaning staff — so a crew is assembled in days, not weeks.

Targeted local job fairs

Active deployment and participation in community-specific recruitment events keeps our bench deep in every market we serve.

Cleaners database

Granular filtering of our historical inventory of experienced environmental services (EVS) and janitorial personnel.

Referral optimization

Structured referral bonus programs that leverage our top-performing cleaners to find peer talent of the same caliber.

Digital & social recruiting

Local digital campaigns aimed directly at professional cleaners, so open roles reach qualified people quickly.

Safety first, always

Chemical handling, PPE and site protocols are covered before anyone steps on the floor — and reinforced on day one.

Services

What we deliver on site

Specialized crews for the work that general cleaning companies are not built for.

Pressure Washing

High-pressure exterior cleaning for concrete, loading docks, walkways, facades, parking structures and equipment pads. Crews are trained on surface-appropriate PSI, chemical selection and water containment so the finish is right and the runoff is handled responsibly.

Concrete & sidewalks Building exteriors Loading docks Post-event cleanup

Green Team — Waste Classification

Dedicated sorting crews that separate landfill, recycling, compost and specialty streams at the source. We stage and service waste stations, guide attendees to the right bin, and keep diversion rates measurable instead of aspirational.

Source separation Recycling & compost Station servicing Diversion reporting
